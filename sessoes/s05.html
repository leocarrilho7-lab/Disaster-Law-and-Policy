S05: International and Comparative Law I: International Frameworks

type: session
  • in this corpusneste corpus: 0 point to it, 0 leave it0 apontam para ele, 0 saem dele

Objetivo (literal, apostila da disciplina, p. 8)

This session surveys the International Law Commission's Draft Articles on the Protection of Persons in the Event of Disasters (2016), the Sendai Framework (2015 to 2030), the Escazú Agreement (2018), the UN Guiding Principles on Business and Human Rights, and the UNFCCC and the Paris Agreement. [...] We also introduce the no-harm principle from Trail Smelter Case and the Stockholm and Rio declarations.

O trecho suprimido é a nota da apostila sobre a ratificação do Acordo de Escazú pelo Brasil, que carrega data de tramitação legislativa e envelhece sozinha. Ela está na fonte, p. 8.

Leitura

A apostila designa duas obrigatórias, o recorte dos arts. 5 a 9 das Draft Articles da CDI e as quatro prioridades de ação do Marco de Sendai, e uma complementar, o capítulo de Lauta no Research handbook on disasters and international law. Nenhuma das três tem verbete ainda, e por isso esta seção não computa bibliografia a partir de pertence_a_sessao, como fazem as sessões vizinhas: o plano de ensino as registra, e a lista aqui é a da apostila.

Perguntas norteadoras (literal, apostila da disciplina, p. 8)

Q1. What does the ILC framework require of affected and assisting States? Are those duties binding?

Q2. How do the four priorities of the Sendai Framework translate into domestic legal obligations?

Q3. Explain the “no-harm principle” from the Trail Smelter Case and the Rio Principle 2. How could it apply to a cross-border disaster?

Formulário facultativo

Há um formulário facultativo desta sessão, anônimo e não avaliado. Ele leva cerca de cinco minutos, alimenta pesquisa sobre como estudantes de direito classificam incerteza, e não altera nota nenhuma: o ponto extra vem do documento enviado pelo E-Class.

This session surveys the International Law Commission's Draft Articles on the Protection of Persons in the Event of Disasters (2016), the Sendai Framework (2015 to 2030), the Escazú Agreement (2018), the UN Guiding Principles on Business and Human Rights, and the UNFCCC and the Paris Agreement. The elided passage is the coursepack's note on Brazil's ratification of the Escazú Agreement, which carries a legislative timetable and dates itself. It is in the source, p. 8.

Reading

The coursepack assigns two required readings, articles 5 to 9 of the ILC Draft Articles and the four priorities for action of the Sendai Framework, and one complementary, the Lauta chapter in the Research handbook on disasters and international law. None of the three has an entry yet, which is why this section does not compute its bibliography from pertence_a_sessao, as neighbouring sessions do: the teaching plan records them, and the list here is the coursepack's.

Optional form

There is an optional form for this session, anonymous and not marked. It takes about five minutes, feeds research on how law students classify uncertainty, and changes no grade: the extra point comes from the document submitted through E-Class.
