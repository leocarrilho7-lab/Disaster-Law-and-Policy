S03: Risk, Uncertainty, Vulnerability and Resilience

type: session
  • in this corpusneste corpus: 7 point to it, 0 leave it7 apontam para ele, 0 saem dele

Objetivo (literal, apostila da disciplina, p. 6)

We separate crisis, disaster, catastrophe, and collapse, and set out a typology that runs from natural to technological and mixed events.

The session then works through the concepts the rest of the course relies on, such as risk against uncertainty (Knight), the risk society (Beck), how people perceive risk (Slovic), rare events with heavy tails (Taleb), and social vulnerability (Wisner and others).

Perguntas norteadoras (literal, apostila da disciplina, p. 6)

Q1. How does Knight tell risk apart from uncertainty? Give a disaster example of each.

Q2. Why do people treat some hazards as more dangerous than the statistics suggest, and what should regulators do about it?

Q3. What is social vulnerability, and why can one hazard produce very different disasters in two countries?
