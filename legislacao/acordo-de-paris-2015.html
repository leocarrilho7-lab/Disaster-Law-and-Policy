A assimetria que a sequencia esconde

Lauta usa os cinco instrumentos como prova de que o direito ja respondeu ao risco de desastre. A sequencia e real e o argumento tem forca, mas quatro dos cinco nao vinculam ✓. O Acordo de Paris e tratado, depositado na UN Treaty Collection e em vigor desde 4 de novembro de 2016. Agenda 2030, Adis Abeba, Sendai e Nova Agenda Urbana sao resolucoes da Assembleia Geral, ou marcos por ela endossados, e resolucao da Assembleia tem natureza de recomendacao.

Isso nao derruba a tese do ensaio, qualifica. O que a sequencia demonstra e que o risco de desastre entrou na agenda normativa global, e nao que tenha ganhado obrigacao exigivel. E a diferenca reaparece com consequencia pratica no parecer do ITLOS: la, a meta de 1,5 grau do Acordo de Paris so vira obrigacao aferivel porque e lida por dentro do art. 194 da Convencao do Mar, que e tratado. O regime climatico precisou da porta de outro tratado para ter dente.

Para o curso, e o teste de leitura mais util deste conjunto: enfileirar instrumentos por data de adocao mede atencao politica; separa-los por forma juridica mede obrigacao.

The asymmetry the sequence hides

Lauta uses the five instruments as evidence that the law has already responded to disaster risk. The sequence is real and the argument has force, but four of the five are not binding ✓. The Paris Agreement is a treaty, deposited with the UN Treaty Collection and in force since 4 November 2016. Agenda 2030, Addis Ababa, Sendai and the New Urban Agenda are General Assembly resolutions, or frameworks endorsed by one, and a General Assembly resolution is in the nature of a recommendation.

This does not defeat the essay's thesis; it qualifies it. What the sequence demonstrates is that disaster risk entered the global normative agenda, not that it acquired an enforceable obligation. And the difference returns with practical consequence in the ITLOS advisory opinion: there, the 1.5°C goal of the Paris Agreement becomes a measurable obligation only because it is read through article 194 of the Law of the Sea Convention, which is a treaty. The climate regime needed another treaty's door in order to have teeth.

For the course this is the most useful reading test in the set: lining instruments up by date of adoption measures political attention; separating them by legal form measures obligation.
